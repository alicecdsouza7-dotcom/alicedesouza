A decade of coordinating art, in and out of the gallery

Alice is an art and cultural project professional with ten years of hands-on experience across museum operations, exhibitions, public art, event production, spatial design and creative project execution. She's coordinated artists, architects, engineers, contractors, vendors, participants and on-site teams, and managed schedules, logistics, procurement and installations from concept through to handover.

Most recently she worked as Art Project Manager with Dr. Subodh Kerkar Studio, delivering museum, public-art, archival and publication projects. She's now based in Dubai and open to roles as Art Project Coordinator, Exhibition Coordinator, Cultural Project Coordinator, Production Coordinator or Assistant Project Manager.
